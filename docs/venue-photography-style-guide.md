# Venue photography style guide

## Visual direction

Use honest, warm-neutral architectural photography with controlled highlights, readable shadow detail, natural greens, and restrained saturation. Keep verticals credible and avoid ultra-wide distortion where possible. Public crops should feel calm and editorial rather than documentary or real-estate-like.

## Required coverage for the next shoot

- Exterior at blue hour or early evening, with the sign illuminated and no identifiable bystanders.
- Dining room at service-ready twilight, photographed before guests arrive.
- Kitchen action with a signed, identifiable staff release: tandoor, plating, and finishing details.
- A clean pass and prep area with all tickets, screens, labels, and private information removed.
- A genuine private-dining or catered-event setup before guests enter.
- Bar details with screens off or out of frame, labels secondary, and shelves tidied naturally.

## Capture and delivery

- Supply original-resolution files without messaging-app recompression when possible.
- Capture landscape and portrait alternatives; leave breathing room for 3:2, 4:3, 5:3, and mobile crops.
- Keep televisions and POS systems off, blurred by depth of field, or outside the composition.
- Obtain explicit approval for every recognizable guest or employee before delivery.
- Do not remove people, change architecture, fill shelves, or manufacture atmosphere with generative editing.

## Publication gate

An image can publish only when ownership is approved, people approval is approved or not applicable, operational information is absent, quality is sufficient for its placement, and its media status is approved.
