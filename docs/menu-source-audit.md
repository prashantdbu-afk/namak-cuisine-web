# Physical menu source audit

Last reviewed: 2026-08-09

## Authority and publication rules

The owner-provided physical-menu photographs are the primary and controlling source for public menu content. Names and category names are preserved as printed, physical-menu prices are publicly displayed, and prices are stored as integer cents. Third-party listings are secondary comparison references only and cannot automatically alter a physical-menu name, description, category, or price.

Spelling normalization has been intentionally disabled by owner direction. Search aliases may help visitors find a printed name, but aliases are internal and never replace the visible name. An item readable in the physical menu may be published without corroboration from delivery platforms.

Ten owner-provided photographs were reviewed: one food-menu cover, six food-menu pages, one bar-menu cover, one spirits/beer spread, and one wine spread. Source filenames are intentionally not exposed in public application content.
