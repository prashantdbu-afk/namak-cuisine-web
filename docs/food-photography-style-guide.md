# Food photography systems

## A — Ivory Coupe Plate (`ivory-coupe`)

Matte ivory, off-white, or very pale stone round coupe plate on warm limestone or soft greige. Use for amuse-bouche, embers, dry starters, kebabs, tandoor dishes, and approved desserts. Gold chargers, blue plates, black slate, tabletop grills, and direct full-banana-leaf presentation do not comply.

## B — Charcoal Indian Vessel (`charcoal-kadhai`)

Matte charcoal, graphite, or dark pewter Indian kadhai/handi on warm limestone or cool pale stone. Restrained brass handles are acceptable. Bright blue, pale green, and unrelated white fine-dining bowls do not comply.

## C — Bread Basket (`bread-basket`)

Natural or neutral serving basket with a consistent black-and-cream liner on a warm neutral background. This system is exclusive to Indian Breads.

All public menu photography uses a 5:3 ratio. Eligibility is based on a manual audit of the actual visible photograph and never inferred from filename, item ID, or category.
