## Summary

- Exact duplication caused the previous homepage repetition: Experience reused a wide floor image and the preview added another dining/bar floor angle.
- Near-identical wide floor views compounded the issue even when filenames differed.
- The revised homepage progresses from overview → food → entrance/table detail → actual bar → seating/bar/exterior preview → Catering → Visit.
- The revised Our Story set is fully separate from the homepage set and balances architecture, food, hospitality, bar, and arrival.
- No source photograph was altered, generated, or upscaled. Rights, production, people, and privacy eligibility remain enforced by the manifest and typed placement validation.
