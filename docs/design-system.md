# Sunlit Spice design system

## Direction

Sunlit Spice presents Namak as contemporary Indian hospitality: calm, warm,
clear, and food-led. Warm neutral surfaces carry most of the interface. Deep
curry-leaf green anchors navigation, actions, and the evening section. Food
photography will eventually supply the strongest color.

## Palette

| Token            | Value     | Use                                 |
| ---------------- | --------- | ----------------------------------- |
| `--canvas`       | `#F8F4EC` | Primary warm page background        |
| `--surface`      | `#FFFDF9` | Raised and alternate light surfaces |
| `--text`         | `#1E2522` | Primary text                        |
| `--text-muted`   | `#69736E` | Supporting text                     |
| `--primary`      | `#1F5A4A` | Buttons, links, navigation emphasis |
| `--primary-dark` | `#152C27` | Evening section and footer          |
| `--pomegranate`  | `#B8484F` | Focus and small active accents      |
| `--saffron`      | `#D9932E` | Decorative media accents only       |
| `--line`         | `#E5DED2` | Rules, borders, and separators      |

Warm light neutrals occupy roughly two-thirds of the homepage. Pomegranate and
saffron remain accents rather than large surfaces or ordinary text colors.

## Typography

Geist is loaded through `next/font` and serves as the primary interface and
display family. The system serif stack appears only for the monogram and any
occasional editorial emphasis. More than 90% of visible typography uses Geist.

- Hero heading: responsive `40–99px`, capped well below the previous oversized
  treatment and approximately `40–46px` on mobile.
- Section headings: responsive `36–77px` on desktop and `37–48px` on mobile.
- Body copy: minimum `16px`, generally `16–20px`.
- Buttons and navigation: compact uppercase Geist with at least 44px targets.

## Media and motif

Neutral local media frames reserve 16:9, 4:5, 3:2, and square placements for
future approved food, cocktail, interior, and hospitality photography. They do
not represent actual dishes. A subtle salt-grain dot texture appears inside
reserved media and map surfaces. No third-party images are loaded.
